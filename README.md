## Public datasets

The `Public_datasets` folder contains two public-use de-identified datasets:

1. `BD_administrativeclinical_records_public.xlsx`: 987 administrative/clinical records.
2. `BD_microbiology_laboratory_records_public.xlsx`: 314 microbiology laboratory records of *Candida* spp. isolates.

These public-use datasets do not include direct patient identifiers or exact dates. Only year-level temporal information and variables required for reproducibility are retained.

The administrative/clinical and microbiology datasets are independent and are not linked at the patient or record level.

## Data privacy

The public datasets were prepared to support reproducibility while protecting patient privacy. Direct identifiers, exact dates, and variables that could facilitate re-identification were removed from the public-use files.

Original restricted source records containing direct identifiers or exact dates are not publicly available due to ethical and legal restrictions.
